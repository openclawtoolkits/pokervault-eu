What Is Simplifying Solvers About?

The core premise of the course is simple: most players use solvers completely wrong.

The typical approach is to run a simulation, try to memorize the output, and move on. The problem is that solvers generate unique strategies for a near-infinite number of board runouts, stack depths, and range configurations. Memorization is a losing battle — the information doesn’t stick, and it doesn’t transfer to new situations.

Galfond’s approach flips this. Instead of asking “what does the solver do here?”, the course teaches you to ask “why does the solver do this?” — and then to compress that reasoning into simple, portable heuristics you can execute at the table without a chart in front of you.

Across the course, you’ll learn:

  • How to simplify multiple solver bet sizes into one executable strategy without meaningfully losing EV
  • How to work backwards from the river to understand the logic of an entire hand
  • How to build unexploitable checking and calling ranges
  • How to convert combo counting into quick percentage-based value betting decisions
  • How different range constructions change strategy, and how to read what each player’s range is “trying to do” in a spot
  • Efficient practice and drilling methods so the concepts actually stick

The BRAIN Framework

The centerpiece of the course is Galfond’s proprietary BRAIN framework — a step-by-step mental model for reading any solver output:

  • B — Board coverage: How well does each range interact with this texture?
  • R — Range & equity advantage: Who has the raw equity and nut advantage?
  • A — Assets & gaps: What key hand classes does each range contain — or miss?
  • I — Incentives: What is each player’s range actually trying to accomplish?
  • N — Nevermind and move on: Knowing when a detail is low-impact noise you can safely ignore.

That last point is underrated and very “Galfond.” A huge amount of wasted study time comes from obsessing over mixed frequencies and tiny EV differences that don’t matter in practice. The BRAIN framework builds in a filter for that — it’s an 80/20 system by design, which is why the course carries the official subtitle The 80/20 Poker Solver System.

Course Structure and Content

The course runs 20+ videos totaling over six hours of dense, concentrated content — no filler, no padded intros. The structure follows a logical progression:

1. The Core Framework. The theoretical backbone: the Value First → Sizing First → River First methodology, range frequency analysis, incentives and range composition, defense strategy, mixed-strategy logic, and the full BRAIN framework.

2. In-Position Aggressor Walkthroughs. Guided solver sessions on flops, turns, and rivers from the aggressor’s seat — learning to spot betting patterns and extract heuristics from raw outputs in real time.

4. Study Optimization. How to structure sessions, drill concepts into muscle memory, and integrate everything into your real-game decision tree.

You also get a downloadable GTO Basics PDF and a Notion study notebook template to organize your solver sessions — a small but genuinely useful addition, since disorganized study is half the problem the course is trying to solve.
