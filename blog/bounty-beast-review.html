Bounty Beast Review: Is This PKO Training Course Worth It?

Progressive Knockout (PKO) tournaments have exploded in popularity across online poker platforms, but many players still approach them with standard MTT strategy—a costly mistake. Today we’re taking a look at Bounty Beast by RaiseYourEdge, a specialized training course designed to help you master bounty tournament strategy.

What Is Bounty Beast?

Bounty Beast is a comprehensive poker training course focused exclusively on Progressive Knockout tournament strategy. Created by RaiseYourEdge, a platform known for its advanced tournament poker training, this course aims to fill the knowledge gap that exists for players transitioning from standard MTTs to the bounty format.

Why PKO Strategy Matters

If you’ve been playing PKO tournaments the same way you play regular MTTs, you’re likely leaving significant EV on the table. The bounty component fundamentally alters:

  • Preflop ranges – Covering players with large bounties requires wider calling ranges
  • ICM considerations – Bounty equity changes risk-reward calculations
  • Stack-off decisions – When bounties are involved, traditional push/fold charts don’t apply
  • Bubble dynamics – The bubble plays differently when half the prize pool is in bounties

What the Course Covers

Bounty Beast addresses the key strategic adjustments needed for PKO success:

Core Concepts

The course breaks down how bounty equity integrates with chip EV and ICM, helping you understand when to deviate from standard tournament play and by how much.

Range Adjustments

Learn how to modify your opening, calling, and 3-betting ranges based on bounty sizes at the table and your relative coverage of opponents.

Exploitative Play

PKO tournaments attract players who either play too tight (not attacking bounties enough) or too loose (overvaluing bounties). The course teaches you to identify and exploit both tendencies.

Late-Stage Strategy

As tournaments progress and bounties accumulate, the strategic adjustments become more pronounced. Bounty Beast covers final table dynamics when bounties represent a substantial portion of remaining prizes.

Who Should Take This Course?

Bounty Beast is ideal for:

  • Regular PKO grinders looking to refine their strategy
  • MTT players expanding into bounty formats
  • Intermediate to advanced players comfortable with basic tournament concepts
  • Anyone who’s felt confused about how much to adjust for bounties

The course assumes you have a foundation in tournament poker, so complete beginners might want to start with fundamental MTT training first.

Course Format and Accessibility

RaiseYourEdge courses typically feature video lessons, hand examples, and supplementary materials. The content is designed to be practical and immediately applicable to your sessions.

Pricing and Value Considerations

Poker training courses are an investment in your game. When evaluating Bounty Beast, consider:

  • How frequently you play PKO tournaments
  • Your current comfort level with bounty strategy
  • The stakes you’re playing and potential ROI improvement
  • Whether specialized PKO knowledge gives you an edge in your player pool

The Bottom Line

PKO tournaments require a specialized skill set that many players lack. If bounty events are a significant part of your schedule, dedicated training can help you capitalize on opponents’ mistakes while avoiding your own.

Bounty Beast offers focused instruction on one of poker’s most popular but misunderstood formats. For players serious about PKO success, having expert guidance on this complex format could be the difference between mediocre and strong results.
