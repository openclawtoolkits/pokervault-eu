GTO LAB Review 2026: Is This the Best MTT Poker Training Site?

If you’re serious about improving your tournament poker game, you’ve probably already heard the name GTO LAB. But does it actually deliver on its promises? We spent time digging into everything the platform offers — coaching content, tools, and value — so you don’t have to guess.

What Is GTO LAB?

GTO LAB (gtolab.com) is a premium online poker training platform built exclusively for tournament (MTT) players. Unlike generalist poker sites that try to cover every format under the sun, GTO LAB has a laser focus: making you a better tournament player by combining elite coaching videos with advanced solver-based training tools.

The platform launched in late 2023 and has quickly become one of the most talked-about names in serious poker study circles — and for good reason.

The Coaching Roster: A Genuine Selling Point

Let’s be blunt — the coaching lineup at GTO LAB is exceptional. We’re not talking about semi-known streamers or mid-stakes regulars. The platform features instruction from some of the biggest names in tournament poker, including Nick Petrangelo, Daniel Dvoress, Jonathan Jaffe, Isaac Haxton, Ben Heath, Leonard Maue, Leon Sturm, Dylan Linde, and Thomas Boivin.

That’s a roster that would make any serious MTT grinder sit up and take notice. These aren’t players dabbling in coaching on the side — they’re actively competing and winning at the highest levels of the game. The coaching library currently spans well over 130 hours of content and continues to grow, covering topics from early-stage hand selection all the way through final table ICM decisions.

Key Features: What You Actually Get

ICM Trainer One of GTO LAB’s standout tools is its full-table ICM trainer — widely considered the most advanced available on any poker training platform. If ICM has always felt like a weak spot in your game (and honestly, it’s a weak spot for most players), this tool alone could justify the subscription cost. You can study how risk premiums shift at different tournament stages, how stack sizes interact with payout pressure, and where most players make costly, avoidable errors.

Postflop cEV Tools In late 2025, GTO LAB launched a fully converged Postflop cEV library after discovering that many popular industry solvers were producing non-converged, unreliable simulations. This is a big deal. If you’ve been studying flop strategy using data from other providers, there’s a real chance some of that information was noise rather than signal. GTO LAB’s postflop tools are built on high-fidelity, accurate data — giving you a cleaner foundation for understanding board textures, range advantages, and bet sizing decisions.

Masterclasses: Tournament Savagery & Multiway Mastery For players who want structured, deep-dive learning rather than a video library to browse, GTO LAB offers standalone masterclasses. Tournament Savagery is a 25-hour course co-created by Daniel Dvoress and another top pro, covering ICM from early stages through final table play in exhaustive detail. Multiway Mastery, taught by Isaac Haxton, tackles one of the most complex and underrated areas of tournament poker: navigating multiway pots with solver-informed thinking. Both are available as standalone purchases for players who don’t want a full subscription.

Who Is GTO LAB Best Suited For?

GTO LAB is built for MTT players who are already past the fundamentals and want to take their game to the next level. If you’re a beginner trying to understand pot odds and basic hand rankings, this probably isn’t where you should start. But if you’re a mid-to-high stakes tournament regular who wants access to elite-level thinking on ICM, bubble play, final table dynamics, and postflop strategy — this platform is hard to beat.

The content covers both online and live tournament formats, applicable across all stake levels, which makes it genuinely versatile for players who move between environments.

GTO LAB vs. Competitors

The most obvious comparison is GTO Wizard, which dominates the broader GTO training market. The key differences worth knowing: GTO LAB offers a full-table ICM trainer that GTO Wizard doesn’t provide, and its coaching content comes exclusively from elite-level tournament professionals rather than a mixed roster of coaches with varying credentials. GTO Wizard has a broader scope for cash game players and beginners; GTO LAB wins decisively on MTT depth and coaching pedigree.

Pricing and Access

GTO LAB offers subscription-based access (Regular and Pro tiers) as well as standalone masterclass purchases. Pro subscribers get full access to the video library. Payment options include standard cards, Luxon, and crypto. The platform also supports subtitles in 10 languages including English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Chinese, Korean, Japanese, and Vietnamese — a nice touch for the global poker community.

The Verdict

GTO LAB has built something genuinely impressive in a relatively short time. The combination of world-class coaching talent, a best-in-class ICM trainer, and now a verified postflop solver library makes it the most complete MTT-focused study platform currently available. It’s not cheap, and it’s not for beginners — but if tournament poker is your primary format and you’re committed to improving, the investment is easy to justify.

Rating: 9/10

Best for: Serious MTT players, mid-to-high stakes tournament grinders, ICM study, final table preparation Not ideal for: Complete beginners, cash game specialists
