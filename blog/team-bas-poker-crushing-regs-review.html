Team Bas Poker Crushing Regs Review: Is This Spin & Go Bundle Worth It?

Most Spin & Go players hit the same wall. You learn the basic ranges, you start beating the recreational players, and then your win rate stalls. The reason is almost always the same: the regulars. They are in a big share of your games, they don’t make the obvious mistakes, and generic strategy advice doesn’t tell you how to beat them.

That’s the problem Team Bas Poker’s Crushing Regs bundle sets out to solve. It packages three of the team’s best-known video series into one product, all focused on playing against competent opponents rather than fish. In this review we’ll break down what’s inside each course, who the bundle suits, where it falls short, and whether it’s worth your money.

What Is the Team Bas Poker Crushing Regs Bundle?

Team Bas Poker (TBP) is a coaching team that specialises in Spin & Go training. The Crushing Regs bundle combines three standalone video series:

  1. Spin & Go Secrets: Winning Strategies Unveiled by TBP head coach Sido_OBC
  2. ART – Analysing Regular Tendencies by Sido_OBC
  3. Mastering ISO Pots by TBP coach Beldarion

Each course tackles a different part of the reg battle. One gives you exploitative lines, one shows you where the regular population is making mistakes, and one focuses on a single high-frequency spot where a lot of EV is won and lost. Together they form a focused study plan rather than a random collection of videos.

Who Are the Coaches?

Sido_OBC is the head coach at Team Bas Poker and the main voice behind two of the three courses. His teaching style blends solver work with real population data: he uses GTO Wizard and range research as a baseline, then shows where actual players deviate from that baseline and how to punish it.

Beldarion is a TBP coach who presents the ISO pot series. His course is the most technical of the three, built around GTO nodelocks and detailed postflop play.

Course 1: Spin & Go Secrets – Winning Strategies Unveiled

This is the broadest course in the bundle and a natural starting point. Sido_OBC combines his experience, range research, GTO Wizard AI and population tendencies to show you lines that most Spin & Go players never find on their own.

Key topics:

  • New and unconventional lines that put opponents in difficult spots
  • Exploits against individual opponents
  • Exploits against entire player pools
  • Using population tendencies to deviate profitably from GTO

The value here is in the “why” behind each deviation. Rather than just telling you to bluff more or less in a spot, the course shows you what the population is doing wrong and builds the exploit from there. That approach carries over to spots the course doesn’t cover directly, which is what separates good training from a list of tips.

Course 2: ART – Analysing Regular Tendencies

If Spin & Go Secrets gives you the lines, ART gives you the evidence behind them. This series is a data-driven study of how regulars actually play, built on population analysis and GTO Wizard.

Key topics:

  • How regulars react on different board textures
  • 3-way scenarios, which are common and often misplayed in Spin & Gos
  • Heads-up scenarios against regs
  • Turning population habits into concrete adjustments

ART is the course that most directly matches the bundle’s name. Understanding how the reg population responds on different textures means you can stop playing every reg as if they were a solver and start targeting their real tendencies. For players who find themselves lost in reg-vs-reg pots, this is probably the most useful of the three.

Course 3: Mastering ISO Pots

Isolation pots come up constantly in Spin & Gos, which makes them one of the most important spots to get right. Beldarion’s series takes you through the full ISO pot journey.

Key topics:

  • GTO nodelocks for isolated pots
  • Advanced flop and turn play
  • Correct use of big ISO sizings
  • Maximising your postflop EV after isolating

This course answers questions a lot of players never stop to ask: Are you isolating with the right hands? Are you using the right sizings? Are you actually realising your edge after the flop? Because ISO pots are so frequent, even small improvements here add up quickly over volume.

How the Three Courses Work Together

The bundle makes sense as a package because each course feeds into the next:

  • ART shows you where the regulars are going wrong
  • Spin & Go Secrets gives you the lines to exploit those mistakes
  • Mastering ISO Pots sharpens a specific spot where you’ll apply them most often

A sensible study order is ART first, then Spin & Go Secrets, then Mastering ISO Pots. Take notes on the population tendencies from ART, then look for how Sido’s exploitative lines target those exact leaks.
