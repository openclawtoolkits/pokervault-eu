While practical application is included, you’ll wade through extensive solver outputs. If GTO theory bores you, the content becomes a slog.

3. Significant Investment

At $999, this represents a major bankroll allocation. For players not yet beating their current stake, that money might generate better ROI through coaching or smaller, targeted courses.

Completing 115+ hours of advanced content will take months. Without disciplined study habits, many students abandon the course halfway through.

5. Facebook Community Requirement

Course questions must be asked in a private Facebook group using your real name—a dealbreaker for players who avoid social media or value privacy.
