Run It Once ‘From the Ground Up’ PLO Course Review (2025): Is It Worth It?

Pot Limit Omaha (PLO) is one of the most exciting and potentially profitable forms of poker today. With its all-action nature and complex strategy, many players are eager to jump in, but a solid foundation is crucial for success. That’s where the Run It Once “From the Ground Up: PLO Edition“ course comes in. In this comprehensive 2025 review, we’ll break down everything this popular PLO course offers to determine if it’s the right investment to kickstart your four-card poker journey.

What Will You Learn in This PLO Course?

Taught by respected PLO pro Thomas Emter, “From the Ground Up” is a massive course featuring 54 detailed video lessons. It’s designed to be a complete roadmap, taking you from a complete novice to a competent, thinking PLO player. The curriculum is logically structured to build your skills progressively.

Core Fundamentals Covered:

  • Bankroll Management for PLO: Learn how to manage your funds to survive the wild swings of the game.
  • Understanding Rake and Variance: Grasp these essential concepts that heavily impact your bottom line in PLO.
  • How to Study PLO Effectively: Develop a process for continuous improvement away from the tables.

In-Depth PLO Strategy Modules:

  • Pre-flop Mastery: Understand starting hand selection, opening ranges, and how to value different hand types.
  • Post-flop Navigation: Learn the intricacies of playing flops, turns, and rivers, both in and out of position.
  • Exploitative Play: Discover how to identify and attack the weaknesses of your opponents to maximize your winnings.
  • Navigating 3-Bet and 4-Bet Pots: Gain confidence in some of the most challenging and high-stakes situations in PLO.

Is This Course Right for You?

This course is specifically designed for two types of poker players:

  1. The Complete PLO Beginner: If you’re new to Pot Limit Omaha, this course is the perfect starting point. It requires no prior knowledge and systematically builds your understanding from the most basic concepts, ensuring you don’t develop bad habits.
  2. The NLHE Player Transitioning to PLO: If you’re coming from a No Limit Hold’em background, you’ll know that PLO is a completely different beast. This course excels at highlighting the critical strategic adjustments you need to make, preventing costly mistakes that many NLHE players make when they first switch games.

While advanced players might find the initial modules to be a review, the sheer depth of content ensures there are valuable insights for almost any player looking to solidify their fundamental PLO strategy.
