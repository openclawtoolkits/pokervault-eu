From The Ground Up MTT Edition Review

When it comes to multi-table tournament (MTT) poker training, finding a course that systematically builds your skills from fundamentals to advanced strategy can be challenging. Run It Once’s “From The Ground Up MTT Edition” promises to do exactly that, offering a structured approach to tournament poker education. In this comprehensive review, we’ll explore whether this course delivers on its promise and who can benefit most from it.

What Is From The Ground Up MTT Edition?

From The Ground Up MTT Edition is a poker training course developed by Run It Once, one of the industry’s most respected poker training platforms. The course is designed to take players through a progressive learning journey, starting with fundamental MTT concepts and advancing through increasingly complex tournament situations and strategies.

Course Structure and Content

The strength of this course lies in its methodical approach to tournament poker education. Rather than jumping between disconnected concepts, From The Ground Up MTT Edition builds each lesson upon the previous one, creating a cohesive learning experience that mirrors how successful tournament players actually develop their skills.

The curriculum covers essential MTT topics including:

Early Game Strategy – Understanding how to build a stack when blinds are low and stacks are deep, including opening ranges, three-betting strategies, and early-game hand selection.

Mid-Game Adjustments – Navigating the crucial middle stages where many players struggle, covering stack-size dependent strategies and bubble dynamics.

Late Game and Final Table Play – Advanced concepts for deep tournament runs, including ICM considerations, short-stack play, and exploitative adjustments against different opponent types.

Tournament-Specific Situations – Detailed analysis of unique MTT scenarios such as satellite strategy, dealing with antes, and adjusting to different tournament structures.

Teaching Methodology

What sets this course apart from random YouTube videos or forum posts is its systematic teaching approach. The instructors break down complex concepts into digestible segments, using real-world examples from actual tournament hands to illustrate key principles. This combination of theory and practical application helps students understand not just what to do, but why certain plays are optimal in tournament contexts.

The course uses a variety of teaching tools including hand history reviews, theoretical explanations, and strategic frameworks that players can apply at the tables immediately.

Who Should Take This Course?

Beginners to Intermediate Players – If you’re new to tournament poker or have been playing recreationally without consistent results, this course provides the structured foundation you need. The “from the ground up” approach ensures you won’t have knowledge gaps in your game.

Cash Game Players Transitioning to Tournaments – Cash game specialists often struggle with tournament-specific concepts like ICM, bubble play, and stack-size adjustments. This course addresses these unique MTT elements comprehensively.

Self-Taught Players Looking to Formalize Their Knowledge – If you’ve learned through trial and error or scattered resources, this course can help organize your knowledge and fill in strategic blind spots.

Pros and Cons

Advantages:

  • Systematic progression from basic to advanced concepts
  • High-quality instruction from experienced tournament professionals
  • Tournament-specific focus rather than generic poker advice
  • Practical application through real hand examples
  • Access to Run It Once’s broader community and resources

Potential Drawbacks:

  • Requires time commitment to work through the full curriculum
  • May cover some basic concepts that experienced players already know
  • Subscription or course fee required for access

Is From The Ground Up MTT Edition Worth It?

For serious players looking to improve their tournament results, From The Ground Up MTT Edition represents a solid investment in poker education. The structured approach eliminates the inefficiency of piecing together strategy from various sources, while the quality of instruction ensures you’re learning proven, theoretically sound concepts.

The course is particularly valuable for players who learn best through systematic, organized content rather than random strategy articles. While experienced tournament grinders might find some sections review material, the comprehensive nature ensures even skilled players will discover new concepts or refine their existing knowledge.

Final Verdict

From The Ground Up MTT Edition successfully delivers on its promise of providing comprehensive, structured tournament poker education. Run It Once has established itself as a premier training site for good reason, and this course exemplifies their commitment to quality instruction.

For players serious about improving their MTT results and willing to invest time in structured learning, this course offers excellent value. The combination of fundamental coverage, advanced strategy, and practical application creates a learning experience that can genuinely transform your tournament game.

Whether you’re looking to take your hobby play to the next level or building skills for professional tournament poker, From The Ground Up MTT Edition provides the educational foundation you need to compete successfully in today’s competitive MTT landscape.
