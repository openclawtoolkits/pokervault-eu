The Core Philosophy: Exploitative vs. GTO

This is the single most important part of this review.

  • Most training sites (like Upswing or Run It Once) focus heavily on GTO. They teach you to play a “perfect,” unexploitable style, which is crucial for beating tough, thinking opponents (mostly online).
  • The Crush Live Poker Blueprint does the exact opposite.

The entire course is built on an exploitative poker strategy. Bart’s philosophy is simple: Why would you try to play a perfectly balanced game against opponents who are playing terribly?

Your average $1/$3 player is not balanced. They are a walking, talking collection of massive leaks.

  • They call way too much.
  • They fold way too much to aggression on the river.
  • They don’t 3-bet nearly enough.
  • They don’t understand position or bet sizing.

The “Blueprint” is a manual for identifying and relentlessly punishing these leaks. It teaches you to deviate from GTO to maximally exploit the mistakes your opponents are making every single hand.
