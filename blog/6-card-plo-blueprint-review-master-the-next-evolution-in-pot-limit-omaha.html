6-Card PLO Blueprint Review: Master the Next Evolution in Pot-Limit Omaha

Complete Course Analysis by PLO Mastermind

If you’re looking to gain an edge in 6-card PLO before the competition catches up, the 6-Card PLO Blueprint by PLO Mastermind might be exactly what you need. This comprehensive training program represents the first structured approach to mastering six-card Pot-Limit Omaha, a variant that’s gaining serious traction in the poker world.

What is 6-Card PLO Blueprint?

The 6-Card PLO Blueprint is an extensive online poker training course featuring over 30 lessons and more than 15 hours of instructional content. Created by professional player Beni, who has achieved double-digit win rates over four years of specializing in 6-card PLO, this course provides a systematic framework for understanding and exploiting this emerging poker format.

Who Should Take This Course?

This training program is ideal for:

  • 4-Card and 5-Card PLO players looking to expand their game and find softer competition
  • Professional poker players seeking new profitable formats
  • Intermediate to advanced players ready to tackle complex poker variants
  • Early adopters who want to establish an edge before solvers catch up

The course assumes some familiarity with PLO concepts, making it most suitable for players who already understand basic Omaha fundamentals.

Course Content Breakdown

Introduction and Foundation

The course begins by establishing whether 6-card PLO fits your playing style and skill set. Beni shares his personal journey to becoming a 6-card PLO specialist, providing valuable context about where to play the game and which poker rooms to avoid.

Preflop Strategy Modules

The theoretical foundation section covers:

  • Hand class tier lists specific to 6-card PLO
  • Position-based raising strategies from UTG and BTN
  • Limping strategies that improved Beni’s win rate
  • Practical application using 5-card PLO Trainer data adapted for 6-card

One of the standout sections includes “10 Truth Bombs About Aces in 6-Card PLO,” which challenges common assumptions players bring from other PLO variants.

Advanced Preflop Concepts

The advanced modules dive deep into:

  • 3-betting and defending against 4-bets
  • Cold calling ranges and hand selection
  • Small blind and big blind defense strategies
  • Common preflop mistakes that cost entire stacks

Postflop Strategy

The postflop section covers both single-raised pots and 3-bet pots comprehensively:

  • Continuation betting in heads-up scenarios
  • Playing as the preflop caller facing aggression
  • Exploiting missed continuation bets
  • The crucial role of donk-betting in 6-card PLO

Player-Type Adjustments

The course concludes with practical advice on:

  • Adjusting to high-VPIP sticky players
  • Heads-up 6-card PLO dynamics
  • Mental game considerations specific to the variance
  • Strategies for dominating aggressive opponents

Key Learning Outcomes

According to the course description, students will learn to:

  1. Transfer GTO knowledge from 4-card and 5-card PLO to the 6-card format
  2. Reduce variance by understanding proper hand selection and risk management
  3. Exploit opponent mistakes that are common in this developing game
  4. Handle aggressive play effectively whether facing or deploying aggression
  5. Understand equity percentages and actual hand strength in 6-card PLO

This creates a window of opportunity for players who study the format systematically to gain a significant edge over recreational players and competitors relying on intuition alone.
