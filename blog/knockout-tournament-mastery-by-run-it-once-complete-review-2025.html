Knockout Tournament Mastery by Run It Once: Complete Review 2025

Overview: Is This KO Tournament Course Worth It?

Knockout Tournament Mastery is a specialized poker training course from Run It Once that focuses exclusively on progressive knockout (PKO) tournament strategy. For players looking to master the unique dynamics of bounty tournaments, this course offers comprehensive instruction on adjusting ranges, calculating bounty equity, and exploiting the specific situations that make KO events different from standard MTTs.

What Is Knockout Tournament Mastery?

This advanced training program teaches players how to adapt their strategy for progressive knockout tournaments, where a portion of each player’s buy-in goes on their head as a bounty. The course covers the mathematical adjustments required when bounties influence optimal play, from preflop ranges to river decisions.

Course Content Breakdown

The training material covers essential PKO concepts including how bounties affect calling ranges, when to apply ICM pressure versus bounty pressure, and how stack sizes interact with bounty values. Students learn to balance tournament equity preservation with bounty collection opportunities.

The course includes real-world examples from online knockout tournaments, showing how theory translates to actual decisions. Hand reviews demonstrate common mistakes players make when they fail to properly account for bounty equity.
