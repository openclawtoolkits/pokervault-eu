Road to Victory Review: Is This Upswing Poker Tournament Course Worth It?

If you’re serious about crushing poker tournaments in 2025, Darren Elias and Nick Petrangelo’s Road to Victory course from Upswing Poker might be exactly what your game needs. After analyzing this comprehensive tournament training program, I’m breaking down everything you need to know before investing in this elite-level course.

What Is Road to Victory?

Road to Victory is Upswing Poker’s flagship tournament poker training course, designed and taught by two of the game’s most accomplished MTT professionals. Darren Elias holds the all-time record for World Poker Tour titles (four and counting), while Nick Petrangelo has amassed over $20 million in live tournament earnings. Together, they’ve created what many consider the most comprehensive tournament poker course available today.

The course targets intermediate to advanced players looking to take their tournament game to the next level, covering everything from early-stage play through ICM-heavy final table decisions.

What’s Included in the Course?

Road to Victory delivers over 50 hours of premium poker training content, structured into detailed modules that cover every phase of tournament play:

Core Curriculum:

  • Preflop ranges and adjustments for all tournament stages
  • Early and middle stage strategy optimization
  • Bubble play and ICM fundamentals
  • Final table dynamics and deal-making
  • Live poker tells and physical reads
  • Mental game and variance management
  • Hand history reviews from major tournaments

The course includes both theoretical lessons and practical hand breakdowns from real high-stakes tournaments, giving you insight into how world-class players actually think through tough spots.

The Instructors: Elias and Petrangelo’s Credibility

Darren Elias brings unmatched WPT expertise and a teaching style that breaks down complex concepts into actionable strategies. His track record speaks for itself—four WPT titles is a feat that demonstrates consistency at the highest levels of tournament poker.

Nick Petrangelo, known in the poker community for his analytical approach and GTO-influenced play, adds a modern, solver-backed perspective to the curriculum. His ability to explain ICM pressure and final table dynamics is particularly valuable for players looking to improve their deep-run results.

The combination of Elias’s proven tournament success and Petrangelo’s technical expertise creates a well-rounded learning experience that addresses both fundamental strategy and cutting-edge concepts.

Key Strengths of Road to Victory

Comprehensive ICM Coverage: The course excels at teaching ICM (Independent Chip Model) concepts that are crucial for tournament success but often poorly understood by recreational players. The final table modules alone justify the course price for serious MTT grinders.

Real-World Application: Unlike purely theoretical courses, Road to Victory emphasizes practical application through actual hand histories from major tournaments. You see how these pros navigate the exact situations you’ll face at the tables.

Production Quality: Upswing Poker maintains high production standards throughout. The video quality, organization, and presentation make complex topics digestible, even during marathon study sessions.

Lifetime Access: Once purchased, you get lifetime access to all course materials, including any future updates. This means you can revisit concepts as your game evolves and benefit from new content additions.

Community Support: Access to the Upswing Poker Lab community allows you to discuss hands, share experiences, and network with other serious tournament players working through the same material.

Potential Drawbacks

Price Point: Road to Victory carries a premium price tag that may be prohibitive for casual players or those just starting their poker journey. While the content quality justifies the cost for serious players, it’s definitely an investment.

Advanced Concepts: Some modules assume a solid foundation in poker fundamentals. Complete beginners might struggle with certain sections without first building their baseline knowledge through more introductory resources.

Volume of Content: With 50+ hours of material, the course can feel overwhelming. Players need to commit significant time not just to watching videos but to implementing concepts at the tables.

Who Should Buy Road to Victory?

This course is ideal for:

  • Intermediate to advanced tournament players looking to sharpen their edge
  • Cash game players transitioning to tournaments
  • Players who regularly deep-run but struggle with final table dynamics
  • Serious MTT grinders willing to invest in their poker education
  • Players with at least a basic understanding of ICM and tournament theory

Not recommended for:

  • Complete poker beginners
  • Casual recreational players
  • Those exclusively focused on cash games
  • Players unwilling to dedicate substantial study time

Does Road to Victory Actually Improve Results?

The honest answer: it depends entirely on your commitment to implementation. The strategies taught by Elias and Petrangelo are proven at the highest levels of tournament poker. However, simply watching videos won’t transform your results—you need to actively study, take notes, and apply concepts at the tables.

Many students report noticeable improvements in their final table performance and decision-making under ICM pressure after completing the course. The edge gained from properly understanding tournament-specific concepts like bubble factors, effective stack sizes, and deal-making can directly impact your ROI in MTTs.

Road to Victory vs. Other Tournament Courses

Compared to alternatives like Jonathan Little’s tournament offerings or free YouTube content, Road to Victory provides more depth and modern, solver-informed strategies. While Jonathan Little’s courses offer excellent value, Road to Victory targets a slightly more advanced audience with its emphasis on high-level play.

Free content from YouTube and poker forums can supplement your learning but lacks the comprehensive, structured approach that Road to Victory provides. For players serious about tournament poker as a long-term pursuit, the investment in structured, professional instruction typically pays dividends.

The Verdict: Is Road to Victory Worth It?

Rating: 4.5/5 Stars

Road to Victory ranks among the best tournament poker training courses available today. The combination of world-class instruction from proven champions, comprehensive ICM coverage, and practical hand analysis makes it a valuable resource for serious tournament players.

The course delivers on its promise to provide elite-level tournament strategy, but it requires commitment. If you’re willing to put in the study hours and have the bankroll to justify the investment, Road to Victory can genuinely elevate your tournament game.

For intermediate players looking to break through to the next level or experienced grinders seeking to refine their final table play, this course represents excellent value despite the premium price point.

Final Recommendation

Buy Road to Victory if: You’re an intermediate or advanced player serious about tournament poker, you have the time to dedicate to proper study and implementation, and you’re looking to master ICM and final table dynamics.

Skip it if: You’re a complete beginner, you’re only casually interested in tournaments, or you’re not prepared to invest both the money and substantial study time required to extract maximum value.

For those committed to tournament poker mastery, Road to Victory provides the roadmap—you just need to put in the work to follow it.
