When it comes to poker training, most players obsess over GTO strategies, range charts, and solver outputs. But what happens when you know the right play but can’t execute it under pressure? What Is the A-Game Poker Masterclass?

The A-Game Poker Masterclass is a comprehensive mental performance program designed specifically for poker players. Created by renowned performance coach Elliot Roe in partnership with Run It Once, this course goes beyond traditional poker strategy to address the psychological barriers that prevent players from performing at their peak.

Unlike standard poker courses that focus solely on technical play, this masterclass combines sports psychology, mindfulness techniques, and practical mental game strategies to help players overcome tilt, maintain focus during long sessions, and develop the mental resilience needed for consistent results.

Who Is Elliot Roe?

Elliot Roe is a performance coach who has worked with some of the biggest names in poker, including Fedor Holz, Chance Kornuth, and Jeff Gross. With over a decade of experience helping players optimize their mental game, Roe has become the go-to expert for elite players looking to gain a competitive edge through mental training.

His approach combines hypnotherapy, visualization, and cognitive behavioral techniques to help players break through mental barriers and achieve peak performance states consistently.

Course Structure and Content

The A-Game Poker Masterclass is divided into multiple modules that progressively build your mental game foundation:

Core Modules Include:

  • Understanding the mental game and why it matters for poker success
  • Identifying your personal mental game leaks and triggers
  • Building pre-session routines that prime you for optimal performance
  • Managing tilt and emotional control during downswings
  • Developing focus and concentration for long grinding sessions
  • Creating effective meditation and mindfulness practices
  • Visualization techniques for confidence building
  • Post-session analysis and mental game review processes

Each module combines video lessons, practical exercises, and guided meditation sessions that you can use during your poker sessions.

What Makes This Course Different?

While generic sports psychology can be helpful, Roe’s program is tailored specifically for the unique challenges poker players face—variance, long-term thinking, game selection anxiety, and the isolation of online grinding.

The course doesn’t just explain concepts; it provides downloadable audio sessions, worksheets, and routines you can implement immediately. The guided meditation tracks are particularly valuable for pre-session preparation.

3. Addresses Real Player Problems

Whether you struggle with shot-taking anxiety, results-oriented thinking, or maintaining discipline during downswings, the course tackles the mental game issues that actually affect your bottom line.
