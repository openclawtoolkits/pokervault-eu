Simple Poker Systems Review (2026): Is BBZ’s Flagship Course Worth It?

Every poker training site has one course that defines it. For Upswing it’s the Poker Lab, for Run It Once it’s From The Ground Up – and for BBZ Poker, it’s Simple Poker Systems. Created by Jordan “BBZ” Drummond, this is the best-selling, highest-rated course in the entire BBZ catalog and the foundation of his whole “Systems” series.

But does it live up to the hype? In this review, we break down exactly what’s inside, who it’s for, where it falls short, and whether it deserves a spot in your study routine in 2026.

What Is Simple Poker Systems?

Simple Poker Systems is a No-Limit Hold’em tournament course built around one core promise: poker is complex, but your strategy doesn’t have to be. Instead of drowning you in solver outputs and chart memorization, Jordan distills modern MTT strategy into 12 plug-and-play systems – clear, repeatable decision rules for the most common and most profitable spots in the game.

The numbers: 15 chapters, 22 videos, and over 9 hours of content, with lifetime access including future updates. The official price is $185 on BBZPoker.com.

What’s Inside: The 12 Systems

The course opens with three preflop lessons – Raise First In, defending vs RFI, and 3-betting vs steals – built on simple heuristics rather than raw chart memorization. Then the systems begin:

  • Systems 1–2: C-betting flops as UTG vs BB and BTN vs BB, including when to check back
  • System 3: BB vs SB limp stabs – a tiny spot that comes up constantly online
  • System 4: River bluffing frameworks, with dedicated videos for BB vs EP and BB vs SB
  • System 5: Barreling medium-strength hands (and why doing it “in error” costs you money)
  • System 6: Check-raising top pair for value
  • System 7: C-bet folding – knowing when to give up
  • System 8: Bet sizing in position
  • System 9: Defending flops with calls and raises vs c-bets
  • Systems 10–11: River value betting and hero calling, each split across two videos
  • System 12: Defending 3-bets out of position

Notice the pattern: the course front-loads the spots you face most often, then spends its second half on the river – the street where the money actually changes hands.

Teaching Style: Why It Works

Jordan’s method is what separates this from most solver-era content. Rather than presenting outputs to memorize, he teaches concepts, then drills them in real time and compares his answers against the solver. Students consistently report the same thing: the material is dense enough that even experienced pros find leaks in it, yet it’s explained simply enough for newer players to follow. One reviewer put it perfectly – the concepts are advanced, but Jordan’s relentless repetition of the main takeaways makes it beginner-friendly by sheer clarity.

Verified student reviews on BBZPoker.com give it a perfect 5.0 rating, with reported winrate jumps like 2bb/100 to 10bb/100 after applying the systems.

Who Should Buy Simple Poker Systems?

  • MTT grinders at any level – this is the target audience, and the course assumes tournament contexts throughout
  • NLHE cash players – the postflop systems (c-betting, river play, hero calling) transfer directly
  • Players tired of chart memorization – the entire philosophy is heuristics over memorization
  • Anyone starting the BBZ ecosystem – this is the explicit prerequisite for Pressure Systems and Passive Systems

Pros and Cons

Pros

  • 12 genuinely actionable systems you can use in your very next session
  • No solver or software required – just the course and a notepad
  • Advanced material made accessible through repetition and drilling
  • Lifetime access with future updates included
  • Perfect 5.0 student rating with documented winrate improvements

Cons

  • At 22 videos it’s leaner than some rival flagships – depth comes from drilling, not volume
  • MTT-first framing means cash players need to do some translation on preflop material
  • Passive lines (checked pots, probe bets) are deliberately left for the sequel course
  • $185 official price is steep if you’re buying multiple BBZ courses at retail

Simple Poker Systems vs the Rest of the BBZ Series

Think of the Systems series as three courses covering one complete game plan. Simple Poker Systems is the aggressor’s foundation: c-betting, value betting, bluffing, defending. Passive Poker Systems flips the script and covers what happens after checks – probe bets, check-backs, and passive river lines. Pressure Poker Systems with Lex Veldhuis is the advanced layer: squeezing, geometric barreling, and river overbets. Start here, then expand in whichever direction your game needs most.

Our Verdict: 9.2/10

Simple Poker Systems earns its reputation. It’s the rare course that improves both a €10 ABI grinder and a seasoned regular, because it doesn’t just show you solver outputs – it rewires how you make decisions. The main criticism (that it skips passive lines) is really just the roadmap for the rest of the series. As a standalone purchase, it’s the best first course in the BBZ catalog and one of the strongest MTT foundations on the market.
