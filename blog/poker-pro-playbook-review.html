What’s Inside Poker Pro Playbook: Chapter-by-Chapter

Chapter 1: Introduction – Before You Begin

A short orientation that frames the course’s philosophy: fix the leaks that cost the most money first, and build habits that make your edge last.

Chapter 2: Professional Mindset & Preparation

This is where Poker Pro Playbook separates itself from most strategy courses. Brian covers:

  • Treating poker like a business — tracking results honestly and making decisions based on data, not feelings.
  • Mental game — staying stable through the brutal variance of tournament poker.
  • Realistic expectations — what win rates and downswings actually look like, so you don’t quit (or overreach) at the wrong moment.
  • Turbo tune-up — adjustments for fast structures, where decisions come quicker and mistakes compound.

Many players skip this kind of material. That’s a mistake. Plenty of technically decent players never make it as pros because they can’t survive the swings or keep a consistent schedule.

Chapter 3: Preflop Opening Strategy

Two lessons, both aimed at money players leave on the table without realizing it:

  • Stop missing easy RFI spots — raise-first-in opportunities that low and mid-stakes regs routinely fold or mishandle.
  • Open shoving more from the SB and BTN — at the right stack depths, shoving wins chips without the complexity of postflop play, and most players do it far too rarely.

Chapter 4: Facing Preflop Opens

Arguably the most valuable chapter for break-even regs:

  • Big blind defense against aggressive openers.
  • BB facing a short-stack shove — a spot that comes up constantly and is often played on gut feel.
  • 3-bet shoving from the blinds vs. the button — a profitable weapon against late-position steals.

Because these situations happen dozens of times per session, even small improvements here move your bottom line quickly.

Chapter 5: Facing Passive Preflop Play

Low-stakes fields are full of limpers. Brian shows how to attack limpers profitably and how to play BB vs. SB completes — a spot most players handle on autopilot despite how often it arises.

Chapter 6: Live Play & Workflow

Two videos that show the theory in action:

  • Live play & explain — Brian plays and talks through his decisions in real time.
  • Operating day to day — how he structures his grind, from game selection to session routine.

Chapter 7: Conclusion

A wrap-up that ties the strategy and the professional habits together into a single plan.
