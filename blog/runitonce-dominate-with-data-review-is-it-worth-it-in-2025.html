What is “Dominate with Data”?

“Dominate with Data” is a premium video course from Runitonce dedicated to Mass Data Analysis (MDA). In simple terms, Patrick “Freenachos” Gerritsen analyzed over 200 million hands from real player pools to find out exactly where the average player (both regs and fish) deviates from GTO.

The course teaches you a three-step process:

  1. Identify Leaks: Pinpoint where population tendencies drift from optimal (e.g., over-folding to turn probes, incorrect c-betting frequencies).
  2. Understand Why: Dive into the reasons why these common mistakes happen.
  3. Build Counter-Strategies: Learn how to build powerful, data-backed counter-strategies to maximally exploit these leaks and increase your poker win rate.

This is an exploitative poker strategy course, but one that is grounded in hard data, not just guesswork.
