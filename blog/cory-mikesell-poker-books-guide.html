FAQ: Cory Mikesell Poker Books

What is the best Cory Mikesell poker book for beginners?

Poker Foundations is the best starting point. It covers essential game theory without requiring advanced poker knowledge.

Are Cory Mikesell’s books suitable for cash games or tournaments?

His books primarily focus on cash game strategy, particularly for online PLO games. The concepts apply to tournaments but aren’t tournament-specific.

What is solver-based poker strategy?

Solver-based strategy uses computer programs (solvers) to calculate mathematically optimal plays in poker situations. Mikesell’s books translate these complex solutions into practical strategies.

Do I need poker solver software to use these books?

No. While Mikesell uses solvers in his research, his books are designed for players without solver access. The Five Card Formula specifically teaches how to study without solvers.

Which book should I read first: PLO 3Bet Pots or Six Card PLO?

Start with PLO 3Bet Pots if you play 4-card PLO. It covers fundamental concepts that apply to all PLO variants. Move to Six Card PLO only if you actively play that variant.

Are Cory Mikesell’s poker books worth the price?

Yes, for serious PLO players. His pioneering work on solver-based strategy and coverage of understudied variants like 5c and 6c PLO provides significant edge over opponents. Given his $1,000/hour coaching rate, his books offer exceptional value.

What’s the difference between 4-card, 5-card, and 6-card PLO?

  • 4-card PLO: Standard variant, most common online and in casinos
  • 5-card PLO: Higher variance, more drawing possibilities
  • 6-card PLO: Extremely high variance, complex hand reading, less common

Each variant requires specific strategy adjustments covered in Mikesell’s respective books.

Can I find Cory Mikesell poker books for free?

Some resources like the App Game Rake Analysis are free. However, purchasing his books supports his continued research and content creation. Sample chapters are sometimes available.
