Curriculum Breakdown

1. Preflop Principles & Stack-Depth Adjustments

Instead of memorizing static cash-game charts that fail at 20–40bb, Frank breaks down tournament-specific preflop construction: handling 2.5x opens, navigating limped vs. raised multi-way pots, and adjusting hand selection across shallow, medium, and deep stacks.

2. Postflop Smart Aggression System

PLO tournaments punish passive play because equities run close and blinds escalate fast. The Smart Aggression module teaches clear heuristics for c-betting, double/triple barreling, and isolating weaker fields without punting off tournament life in low-SPR traps.

3. ICM Mastery & Payjump Pressure

ICM in PLO behaves very differently from NLHE because hands rarely have 80/20 equity preflop. This section covers exact bubble factors, big-stack bullying vs. medium-stack survival, and final-table adjustments where a single pot swings massive equity.

4. PKO & Mystery Bounty Adjustments

Modern PLO MTT schedules are packed with Progressive Knockouts and Mystery Bounties. You get concrete frameworks for bounty equity conversion, wider isolation ranges, and postflop stack-off thresholds when covering opponents.

5. Dual-Instructor Session Reviews

Several deep-run hand reviews feature both Christopher Frank and JNandez dissecting real spots together, giving you both a solver-grounded tournament perspective and a lifetime PLO specialist's intuition.
