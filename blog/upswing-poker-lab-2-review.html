Upswing Lab 2.0 Review: The Most Complete No-Limit Hold’em Training Course in 2026?

If you’re serious about improving your poker game, you’ve almost certainly heard of Upswing Poker. Their flagship training product — the Upswing Lab — has been one of the most talked-about coaching programs in the industry for years. Now, with the launch of Lab 2.0, the platform has been completely rebuilt from the ground up. But is it worth your money? We dug deep to find out.

What Is Upswing Lab 2.0?

Upswing Lab 2.0 is a subscription-based No-Limit Hold’em training membership designed to take players from wherever they currently are and move them steadily toward elite-level play. Taught by high-stakes crusher and world-class poker coach Uri Peleg, the course provides a clear, structured path to poker mastery — whether you’re a beginner or an experienced reg looking to sharpen your edge.

This isn’t just a refresh of the original Lab. Lab 2.0 is built as a smart, adaptive learning path: a placement quiz ensures you begin exactly where you need to, with no retracing of material you’ve already mastered. That alone sets it apart from the one-size-fits-all approach that plagues most poker training sites.

Who Is Uri Peleg?

The head coach behind Lab 2.0 deserves a proper introduction. Uri Peleg is the founder of staking company Guerrilla Poker and a world-class strategist with over $2 million in personal winnings. His students have collectively earned more than $25 million at the tables. Perhaps most impressively, he also holds a Magic: The Gathering World Championship title — a testament to his mastery of complex strategic thinking.

Members consistently praise his ability to simplify complex concepts without ever dumbing them down, noting it’s a rare skill to make things clear while still preserving their depth.

What’s Inside Upswing Lab 2.0?

Structured Learning Path

Lab 2.0 is built around a progressive curriculum that avoids the common trap of dumping hundreds of hours of unorganized content on members. You follow a Launch Pad before entering the more advanced Mastery Path — progressively structured from foundations to elite exploits, with weekly additions including new lessons and Max Exploit Academy content.

Elite Video Training

The core of the course is high-quality video instruction from Uri and a hand-selected team of coaches. Members report that even content on familiar topics can deliver genuine “aha” moments — which is the hallmark of truly exceptional teaching.

Interactive Tools & Quizzes

Unlike passive video courses where you sit and watch, Lab 2.0 includes interactive practice tools and quizzes that let you test your knowledge, receive feedback, and pinpoint your weaknesses.This active learning approach accelerates improvement significantly.

Preflop Prodigy App

One of the standout additions in Lab 2.0, the Preflop Prodigy App lets you master expert preflop ranges on any device, for both cash games and tournaments. This is the kind of tool that turns abstract theory into muscle memory.

Live Coaching & Weekly Q&As

Members get access to weekly Q&A sessions and live strategy breakdowns for real-time insights — something most pre-recorded courses simply can’t offer.

Private Discord Community

Lab 2.0 members get access to Upswing’s private Discord server, where you can ask questions, discuss strategies, and connect with fellow grinders working through the same content.The community also features a member blog section where you can track and share your progress — think of it as a modern version of the classic poker forums, but with actual coaches reading and responding.

Pricing

Lab 2.0 is offered as a monthly membership. The membership is priced at $99/month, though you can save significantly by committing to a six-month plan at $399 or a full-year membership at $699. For those on the fence, there is no free trial — but Upswing does back the course with a 30-day satisfaction guarantee for first-time members.

Who Is Lab 2.0 Best For?

Lab 2.0 is an excellent fit for:

  • Beginners who want a structured, no-confusion path to becoming a solid poker player
  • Intermediate grinders stuck at micro or small stakes who need to identify and fix their leaks
  • Live poker players tired of bleeding money against better-prepared opponents
  • Online regs looking to add exploitative depth on top of a sound GTO baseline

It’s worth noting that for medium and high-stakes players, some of the foundational content may feel thin, though the Max Exploit modules and advanced preflop training ensure there’s still genuine value for more experienced players.

What Members Are Saying

The feedback from the Lab 2.0 community has been overwhelmingly positive. One member described it as the best poker course they had seen, adding that access to Uri directly in the private Discord — including personalized answers to game-specific questions — alone justified the investment.

Another member noted that the advanced preflop module inside Lab 2.0 was superior in quality and value to a $499 preflop product they had purchased elsewhere.

The Verdict

Upswing Lab 2.0 is one of the most complete, well-organized poker training memberships available in 2026. The combination of an adaptive learning path, elite coaching from Uri Peleg, interactive tools, live Q&As, and an engaged Discord community makes it stand out in a crowded field. The pricing is fair for the volume and quality of content on offer, especially if you commit to a longer membership.

If you’re looking for a single resource to meaningfully move the needle on your No-Limit Hold’em results, Lab 2.0 is the strongest recommendation we can make.
