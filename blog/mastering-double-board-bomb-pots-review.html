What Are Double Board Bomb Pots?

Before diving into the course review, let’s quickly cover the basics. In a double board bomb pot, all players put in a predetermined amount (usually 2-5 big blinds) before seeing their cards. No preflop action occurs, and then two separate boards are dealt. Players can win both boards, split the pot, or lose entirely. The result? Enormous pots with complex decision trees that can make or break your session.
