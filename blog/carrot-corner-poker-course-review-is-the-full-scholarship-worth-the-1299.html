Carrot Corner Poker Course Review: Is “The Full Scholarship” Worth the £1,299?

Thinking of buying the Carrot Corner “Full Scholarship” poker course? Our in-depth 2025 review breaks down Pete Clarke’s cash game course, the pros, cons, and who should really buy it.

We’ve all been there. You’re grinding the micro-stakes, watching your graph look more like a seismograph, and you stumble upon Pete “Carroters” Clarke’s YouTube channel. His explanations are clean, his logic is sound, and he makes GTO concepts seem… well, simple.

Then you see it: The Carrot Poker School and its premium bundle, “The Full Scholarship.”

But at a hefty £1,299.00, a major question looms: Is this the investment that finally gets you out of 5NL, or is it just an expensive way to be told to “check-raise your nut flush draws”?

As a poker blog dedicated to finding the best training, we dove in to see what’s under the hood. Here is our official Carrot Corner Poker Course review.

First, What is “The Full Scholarship”? (Hint: It’s Not Free)

Let’s clear this up immediately. “The Full Scholarship” is the branded name for the all-inclusive bundle of Pete Clarke’s cash game courses. It is not a free program but rather a package deal that saves you about £700 compared to buying each “Grade” separately.

So, what do you get for your money?

This is, without a doubt, a comprehensive curriculum. It’s designed to function like a university degree for No Limit Hold’em cash games.

The Good: Why This Course Shines

1. Pete Clarke is a World-Class Teacher

This is the single most important point. There’s a huge difference between a “crusher” and a “teacher.” Pete Clarke is an elite teacher. He has a rare talent for breaking down incredibly complex topics (like node-locking or range construction) into simple, digestible, and memorable concepts. You won’t just learn what to do; you’ll finally understand why you’re doing it.

2. A True A-to-Z Structured Curriculum

Forget random “Video of the Week” packs. The Carrot Poker School is a linear, structured program. It builds a solid foundation in Grade 1 and then layers complexity on top. For anyone who has felt lost, overwhelmed, or “stuck” in their poker journey, this academic structure is a godsend for plugging leaks.

3. The “Grade E” Exploitative Edge

While Grades 1-3 focus on building a balanced, GTO-style “baseline,” Grade E is where the rubber meets the road. This course uses mass data analysis to show you how the actual player pool deviates from GTO and provides concrete strategies to exploit those leaks.

4. A Genuinely Valuable Community

The private Discord access is a massive, underrated perk. It’s filled with other serious students on the same journey. The hand-history discussions are high-level, and the community is supportive, active, and moderated by coaches.

The Bad: Potential Dealbreakers to Consider

No review is honest without a balanced look. Here are the potential drawbacks.

1. The “Coach vs. Crusher” Debate

This is the biggest criticism of Carrot Corner. Pete Clarke is a full-time educator, not a high-stakes pro who plays 100,000 hands a month. He doesn’t provide a recent, high-volume graph. For players who believe you should only learn from someone actively beating 500NL, this is a major sticking point.

Our take? It’s a weak argument. Your university physics professor doesn’t need to be a full-time NASA astronaut to teach you physics. Pete’s job is to teach, and he’s one of the best.

2. The £1,299.00 Price Tag

This is a massive investment, period. For a 10NL player with a $400 bankroll, this course costs more than three full buy-ins at the next stake. The price can be a significant barrier to its target audience.

3. Not for Mid-Stakes Grinders

If you are already a solid, winning player at 50NL or 100NL, you will likely find much of this course (especially Grades 1 and 2) to be too fundamental. You may be better off with a subscription site, a solver, or private coaching.

Final Verdict: Is the Carrot Corner Poker Course Worth It?

After a thorough review, here is our conclusion:

The Carrot Corner “Full Scholarship” is one of the best foundational poker courses on the market, but it is not for everyone.

It is an A+ product for its specific target audience. Think of it as investing in a 4-year university degree. It’s expensive, it’s comprehensive, and it’s designed to give you a complete education from the ground up.

🧑‍🎓 You SHOULD Buy This Course If…

  • You are a beginner, micro-stakes (2NL-25NL), or struggling low-stakes player.
  • You feel overwhelmed by GTO and need a clear, structured way to learn.
  • You learn best in an academic, A-to-Z format with homework and exams.
  • You value a strong, supportive community (Discord).

⛔ You SHOULD SKIP This Course If…

  • You are already a consistent winning player at 50NL or higher.
  • You have a very small bankroll (this course is a massive part of it).
  • You are looking for “pro secrets” and “solver-killer” tricks, not foundational theory.
  • You only want to learn from a “crusher” with a public graph.
